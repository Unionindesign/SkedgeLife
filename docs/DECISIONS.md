## New open questions surfaced while deciding the above

- **Is "student" vs. "instructor" the right hard line long-term?** Raised 2026-08-22 when scoping the tier decision — the product currently draws a firm role boundary, but it may need revisiting (e.g. someone who is both, or a single "user" identity with instructor capabilities layered on via upgrade rather than a separate role). Flagged as a real open question, not resolved — revisit before Phase 1 schema work locks in `Instructor` vs. `StudentProfile` as fully separate tables.
- **Handle-priority mechanism**: what actually happens if a student claims `/u/michelle` and an instructor named Michelle later wants that handle? Needs a real policy (reservation window, role-based override, etc.) before the slug system ships.
